How to apologize to your partner so it actually lands

You said sorry, and somehow it made things worse. A real apology isn't about the word "sorry". It's about showing you understand what it was like for them. Here's how, with words for your girlfriend, boyfriend, husband or wife.

Updated 2 November 2026By Utku, founder of Deesca7 min read
Short answer

A real apology has four parts: name exactly what you did, show you understand how it affected them, take responsibility without "but", and say what you'll do differently. Keep it short, mean it, and don't ask for instant forgiveness. Then follow through, because the change is what makes the apology believable.

The four parts of a real apology

1. Name what you did, specifically

"I'm sorry for everything" sounds like you're not sure what you did. "I'm sorry I made a joke about your job in front of your friends" shows you know.

2. Show you understand the impact

"That must have felt humiliating, especially in front of people you care about." This is the part most apologies skip, and the part that matters most.

3. Take responsibility, no "but"

"I was stressed, but…" turns an apology into a defence. Your reasons can come later, if they ask.

4. Say what you'll do differently

"Next time I'm in a bad mood, I'll say so instead of taking it out on you." A specific promise, then keep it.

Apology examples

To your girlfriend or wife

  • "I'm sorry I forgot our plans and left you waiting. You'd been looking forward to it all week, and I made you feel like an afterthought. I've put our plans in my calendar with reminders so it doesn't happen again."
  • "I'm sorry I raised my voice. You were telling me something that mattered and I made it about me. I'd like to hear it again, properly."

To your boyfriend or husband

  • "I'm sorry I criticised you in front of your family. That was embarrassing for you, and it wasn't fair. If something bothers me, I'll bring it up when it's just us."
  • "I'm sorry for what I said about your friends. I was upset about something else and took it out on them. They matter to you, and I'll be more careful."

When you're not sure what you did wrong

  • "I can tell I hurt you, and I don't fully understand how yet. I'd really like to. Can you tell me what it was like for you?"Honest, and better than a vague "sorry for whatever I did".

How to apologize over text

In person is usually better, but sometimes a text is the only door that's open. Keep it short and save the longer conversation for later:

  • "I'm sorry about earlier. I was unfair to you and I know it hurt. I'd like to talk properly tonight, if you're up for it."
  • "I've been thinking about what you said, and you were right that I wasn't listening. I'm sorry. Can I call you later?"

Avoid long paragraphs re-explaining your side, several messages in a row while they're quiet, and emojis that make it look like a joke.

What never to say

  • "I'm sorry you feel that way."Apologises for their feelings, not your actions.
  • "I'm sorry, but you…"The "but" cancels everything before it.
  • "I already said sorry, what more do you want?"Treats the apology as a payment, not a repair.
  • "Can we just move on?"Pushes them to drop something that still hurts.

If they don't accept it

Forgiveness takes as long as it takes. Don't argue about whether your apology was good enough. Say: "I understand. I'm not expecting it to be fine straight away. I'll show you it's different." Common questions

How do you apologize to your partner after a fight?

Name what you did, acknowledge how it made them feel, take responsibility without "but", and say what you'll do differently. Keep it short and follow through.

How do I apologize to my girlfriend for hurting her feelings?

Be specific about what you said or did, show you understand why it hurt her, avoid excuses, and tell her what you'll change. Then give her time instead of asking for instant forgiveness.

Is it okay to apologize over text?

Yes, as a first step. Keep it short and sincere, and suggest talking properly in person or on a call.

What if my partner won't accept my apology?

Give them time and show the change through what you do. If the same apology keeps coming up, talk about the underlying argument rather than apologising again.
