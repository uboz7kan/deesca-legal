Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026. Operated by Deesca, an independent developer ("we", "us"). Contact: deescaapp@gmail.com.

Who we are

Deesca is a mobile app that helps couples move from conflict to clarity using AI-assisted mediation, coaching, and reflection.

Data we collect

  • Account: when you first open the app we create an account with a random identifier and no email. If you choose to link it, we also store your Sign in with Apple identifier or your email address (passwordless sign-in).
  • You provide: your name and your partner's name/details, relationship status, onboarding answers (including how you heard about Deesca), the text of arguments and reflections you enter, screenshots you upload, and voice recordings you make.
  • Generated: AI analyses (mediation reports, cooldown reports, red-flag analyses), a per-user "relationship memory" summary, mood/journal entries, and usage counts.
  • Automatic: basic device/diagnostic data, crash reports, and product-usage events (for example, that a screen was opened or a plan was selected), tied to your random account identifier. These never include the text you write, transcripts, screenshots, names, or your email address.
  • Purchases: subscription status via Apple/RevenueCat. We never receive your full payment-card details.

How we use it

To provide and personalize the app, generate AI guidance, remember context across sessions, enforce fair-use limits, process subscriptions, and maintain security. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Audio

Voice recordings are sent for transcription, then discarded — we do not retain the raw audio. The resulting text is stored as your session/journal content.

AI processing & your explicit consent

Deesca's core features work by sending the content you choose to submit to trusted third-party AI providers, which generate your guidance. Before any of your content is sent to these providers, we ask for your explicit consent inside the app — you are shown a clear disclosure of what is sent and to whom, and you must agree before Deesca proceeds. You can withdraw at any time by deleting your account (see “Your rights & choices”).

What we send, and to whom:

  • Anthropic (“Claude”) — receives the text you type, the screenshots you upload, and the transcripts of what you say, in order to generate mediation reports, coaching replies, red-flag analyses, and cooldown reports.
  • OpenAI — receives the voice recordings you make, solely to transcribe them into text. The audio is discarded immediately after transcription; we do not retain it.

We send only the relationship content you choose to submit. We do not send your email address or other account identifiers to these AI providers, and all transmission is over encrypted connections.

Equal protection. We only share your data with third parties that are contractually bound to protect it to at least the same standard described in this policy. Anthropic and OpenAI process your data under their commercial API terms, which do not use your content to train their models. Your content is never sold and is never used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Other service providers (sub-processors)

  • Supabase — our own backend: database, authentication, storage, and hosting (your rows protected by row-level security).
  • RevenueCat + Apple — subscription management. We never receive your full payment-card details.
  • PostHog (EU hosting) — product-usage analytics, so we can see which parts of the app work and where people get stuck.
  • Sentry (EU hosting) — crash and error reports, so we can fix what breaks.

Storage, security, retention

Data is stored on Supabase with row-level security so each user can access only their own rows. We keep your data while your account is active and delete it on account deletion.

Your rights & choices

  • AI processing consent: you agree in-app before any content is sent to our AI providers, and you can withdraw at any time by deleting your account.
  • Access/export your data: contact us.
  • Delete everything: Settings → Delete account permanently erases your account and all associated data (sessions, journals, coach memory, partner details).
  • Manage subscription: through your Apple ID settings.

Children

Deesca is for people aged 16 and over. During setup we ask whether you are 16 or older, and anyone who answers that they are younger cannot continue. We do not save that answer to your account; we only record, as a usage event, that the under-16 stop was shown. If you believe someone under 16 has given us their data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new date above.
